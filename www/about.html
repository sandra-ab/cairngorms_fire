This application uses NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS) to visualise the spatial and temporal evolution of the Cairngorms wildfire that started on July 15th, 2026.

The analysis is based on 3 near-real-time satellite products , each with a resolution of approximately 375 metres. These sensors detect thermal anomalies associated with fire activity, rather than directly mapping burnt ground.

Satellite data were obtained from the FIRMS service and aggregated on a daily basis. For each location, the maximum Fire Radiative Power and the median detection confidence (not all observations are as reliable) were retained.

To support visualisation and analysis, observations were transferred to a 100m grid. Grid cells are classified as containing fire activity where at least 50% of the FIRMS observation footprint overlaps the cell.

This app refreshes twice a day.
